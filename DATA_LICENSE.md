# Data licence

Tables, protocols, locked predictions, logs and other outputs produced in this repository are
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite the paper (see `README.md`).

Third-party data keep their own terms and are not covered by this licence:

- The culture reference (`reference/culture_ref_v4/`) is derived from GenBank records deposited by
  Stefani et al. (2025); cite that paper when using it.
- MaarjAM, UNITE, the Delavaux LSU database and LongGloDB are not redistributed here; only their
  checksums are given, in `external_refs/CHECKSUMS.sha256`.
